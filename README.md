Every month, I'll be posting the planetary configurations (longitudes) and their transits as reflected in the D3 (Drekkana) and D4 (Chaturthamsa) charts, along with my interpretation of the results.
About D3 (Drekkana):
The D3 chart divides each sign into three parts of 10° each. It is primarily used to study siblings, courage, communication, and one's inherent drive/initiative. Transiting planets through sensitive points of the D3 can indicate periods affecting relationships with siblings, short journeys, and personal valor.
About D4 (Chaturthamsa):
The D4 chart divides each sign into four parts of 7°30' each. It relates to fortune, property, fixed assets, vehicles, home, and general comfort/happiness (sukha). Transits activating key D4 points often correlate with events involving real estate, vehicles, or shifts in domestic stability.
